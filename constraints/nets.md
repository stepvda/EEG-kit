## Flagged nets (reviewed, no open question)

* **CMP_RAW** -- crosses the zone split by declared design exception (comparator output to a 3V3 GPIO through R83/D23) -- confirmed intentional in design.py comments
* **HP_TAP** -- allowed in both zones: an audio-band analogue signal that must reach the codec (digital zone) and an envelope detector (analogue zone).  Deliberate, per RFQ E-11/E-12.
* **ROOM_PRE** -- allowed in both zones: an audio-band analogue signal that must reach the codec (digital zone) and an envelope detector (analogue zone).  Deliberate, per RFQ E-11/E-12.
* **VOICE_PRE** -- allowed in both zones: an audio-band analogue signal that must reach the codec (digital zone) and an envelope detector (analogue zone).  Deliberate, per RFQ E-11/E-12.

## Known documentation defects

* router.py's docstring claims SPI is length-matched within 10 mm; nothing measures it.  Electrically irrelevant at <= 8 MHz on a 150 mm board, but the claim should be removed or the check added.
* No length-match tolerance is stated anywhere for the USB pair; the fabrication notes call the impedance figure informative and waive the coupon.

ART-LBL-04 TAG SIZE -- THE SPECIFIED TAG CANNOT CARRY THE SPECIFIED TYPE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PKG-EEG-015 section 4.2 fixes the bay tag at 60 x 12 mm and section 2.3
asks for an 8 mm cap height. Not one of the nine legends fits on one line:
the least demanding needs about 88 mm of tag and the worst about 320 mm.
The tags are drawn at the size section 4.2 fixes, at the largest cap height that
fits on at most two lines. The last column is the tag width that would carry
that legend at 8 mm on at most two lines.

  legend                                            cap mm  lines  8 mm needs
  HELMET HM-01                                        4.89      1         52
  HEADPHONES                                          5.36      1         88
  POD-P1 ENCLOSURE                                    4.62      2         76
  BOOM MICROPHONE                                     4.62      2         86
  SPARE CUPS + KEYLESS SPARES                         4.35      2        107
  SPARE CELL -- DEPOT ONLY, EMPTY IN CIRCULATION      2.68      2        171
  CONSUMABLES                                         4.86      1         96
  EAR CLIPS + EMG LEADS                               4.62      2        110
  CABLES + CHARGER                                    4.62      2         63

A tag of about 171 x 21 mm carries every legend at 8 mm on at most
two lines. That is a PROPOSAL. Changing the tag size changes section 4.2, and
PKG-EEG-015 section 9 carries it as an open item for the programme to settle.

WHAT HAS NOT BEEN VERIFIED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing here has been printed, applied, wiped with IPA or read by a barcode
verifier. The Data Matrix encoder is checked against the ISO/IEC 16022 worked
example and by decoding its own symbols; that proves the encoder, not a printed
label. PKG-EEG-015 section 4.1 requires ISO/IEC 15415 grade C or better after
50 wipes with 70 % IPA, and that first-article verification is what releases
this artwork for use.

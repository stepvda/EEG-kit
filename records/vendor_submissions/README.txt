=====================================================================

WHY THIS IS A SEPARATE DIRECTORY, AND NOT A SUBMISSION-SHAPED TEST RECORD

The rest of `records/` is one thing: the per-unit test record that TST-EEG-004 section 12
specifies, generated by `records/make_records.py` and validated against a schema. A vendor
submission is not that. It is a programme event -- this pack, to this vendor, against this
ticket, on this date -- and it is written once and not regenerated, because re-running a
generator cannot change what a manufacturer already received.

Keeping the two apart means `make_records.py` stays the single writer of everything else
in `records/`, and `records/README.txt` stays true.

WHAT A RECORD HERE MUST CARRY

  * the vendor, the desk and the vendor's own ticket number;
  * every file sent, with its SHA-256 **read from the pack, never transcribed**;
  * the date;
  * what the quotation is FOR -- and in particular, when it is budgetary rather than
    firm, the fact that it is priced against geometry that has been withdrawn;
  * every deviation between the specification and what the vendor's form could express,
    because a form that has no control for a requirement does not make the requirement
    go away;
  * whether it was actually SENT. A pack that was built and filled but not submitted is
    not a submission, and the record says so in terms.
